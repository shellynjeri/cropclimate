About CropClimate 🌱

CropClimate is a web-based agricultural vulnerability prediction prototype designed to demonstrate how climate conditions can affect agricultural production.

Our Objectives

  • Identify possible agricultural risks.
  • Analyse rainfall and temperature conditions.
  • Evaluate sample soil moisture levels.
  • Provide adaptation recommendations.
  • Help demonstrate climate-aware agricultural planning.
Prototype notice

This version uses illustrative sample data and demonstration calculations. It is not connected to live climate data or a validated prediction model.
